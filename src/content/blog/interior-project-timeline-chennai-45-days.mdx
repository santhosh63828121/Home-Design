The most persistent anxiety before signing an interior contract is the same everywhere: *"How long will it actually take?"* Delays don't just push back a moving date — they extend rental costs, disrupt your life, and turn an exciting project into a stressful one.

We've engineered a synchronised modular execution process that delivers complete, high-end residential interiors within a **45-day handover timeline** — without cutting corners on precision.

<Callout>
**Operational note.** Our 45-day completion guarantee applies to **standard 2BHK and 3BHK residential modular woodwork packages**, and the clock starts on final design sign-off, material selection lock and site hand-off. Turnkey civil modifications or specialty handcrafted masonry may adjust this roadmap.
</Callout>

## The true cost of delays

When an interior firm misses its date, the consequences cascade:

- **Dual financial burden** — paying pre-EMI or home-loan instalments *and* extended rent.
- **Postponed milestones** — auspicious housewarming (*Griha Pravesham*) dates rescheduled.
- **Unchecked escalations** — extended timelines inflate security, storage and oversight fees.

## The 45-day execution blueprint

By shifting heavy woodwork fabrication **off your site** and into a manufacturing facility, the timeline compresses dramatically.

| Phase | Days | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Design freeze & site scan** | 1–5 | Ultra-precise laser measurement of the empty apartment. Layout finalised, space planning confirmed, lifestyle requirements locked. |
| **3D renders & material selection** | 6–10 | Photorealistic renders shared. Final finishes selected — laminates, textures, handle-less profiles, soft-close hardware. |
| **Precision factory manufacturing** | 11–25 | Approved designs converted to CAD and manufactured (cutting, drilling, 360° edge banding) while your site stays largely dust-free. |
| **Parallel on-site infrastructure** | 26–35 | Electrical changes, LED wiring, false-ceiling framing, floor protection and primer — executed *in parallel* with factory production. |
| **Rapid modular assembly** | 36–42 | Factory-finished units arrive flat-packed and are assembled quickly on site using pre-drilled, square components. |
| **Deep clean, snagging & handover** | 43–45 | Final QC (alignment, runner tests, edge sealing), deep cleaning, snag resolution and the handover walkthrough. |

## How we hold the schedule

We don't hit 45 days through rushed carpentry. We hit it through logistics:

1. **Elimination of site carpentry.** Moving the bulk of construction to the factory removes delays from power cuts, manual error, material hold-ups and labour absence.
2. **Dedicated project supervision.** Every flat gets a single point-of-contact project engineer who prevents scheduling conflicts between electricians, painters and assemblers.
3. **Pre-cleared supply networks.** Direct relationships with top-tier brands mean raw materials bypass standard distributor delays.

## What legitimately alters the timeline

We flag these early rather than surprising you later:

- **Complex civil changes** — demolishing masonry walls, altering plumbing paths, laying entirely new flooring.
- **Mid-project revision loops** — changing laminate selections or cabinet placement *after* the factory run has started.
- **Specialised artisanal finishes** — multi-coat liquid PU, duco, or detailed hand-carving.

## How you can help hold the date

The fastest completions are always a partnership:

- **Freeze designs early.** Spend the extra time in the first ten days perfecting the layout, so changes aren't needed during production.
- **Approve materials promptly.** Wallpapers, light fixtures and paint tones.
- **Secure society clearances.** Get RWA approval early for working hours, freight elevator use and vendor entry passes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a 45-day timeline mean the work is rushed?
The opposite. Traditional site carpenters take months because they cut and paste by hand with handheld tools. We use computer-controlled machinery that cuts panels to sub-millimetre accuracy in seconds. The speed comes from **technology, not from rushing labour**.

### What happens if you exceed the 45 days?
Our project agreements include clear timeline clauses. If a delay occurs **exclusively due to an operational slip on our end**, we assume accountability as set out in your contract.

### Can I visit the site while the clock is running?
Absolutely. We also send regular photo and video milestones straight to your phone, so you can track progress without fighting Chennai traffic.

## Final thoughts

A residential interior project shouldn't feel like an endless waiting game. Factory pre-fabrication, parallel scheduling and rigorous project management mean you can step into your home exactly when you planned to.
