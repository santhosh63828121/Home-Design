The shutter finish is the most visible — and one of the most cost-defining — decisions in any kitchen or wardrobe. For most Chennai homes the choice comes down to **laminate** or **acrylic**. Neither is "better" in the abstract; they're suited to different priorities. Here's an honest comparison so you can pick with confidence.

## The short version

- **Laminate** is the practical all-rounder: durable, low-maintenance, huge range of looks, and the most cost-effective.
- **Acrylic** is the premium look: a glossy, mirror-like finish with real depth of colour — more striking, more delicate, and more expensive.

## What they actually are

**Laminate** is a thin printed-and-pressed sheet bonded onto the shutter board. Modern laminates come in matte, suede, textured-wood and high-gloss versions, in over a thousand colours and patterns.

**Acrylic** is a coloured acrylic layer that gives a seamless, high-gloss, glass-like surface. The colour sits *in* the material, which is what gives acrylic its signature depth and shine.

## Looks

If you want a deep, reflective, showroom shine — especially in solid colours — acrylic is hard to beat. It makes a kitchen feel luxurious and is stunning in handleless designs.

Laminate is more versatile. Want a realistic woodgrain, a soft matte, or a textured stone look? Laminate does all of it, and a good matte laminate is both forgiving and very current.

## Durability and daily life

This is where laminate quietly wins for busy family kitchens:

| | Laminate | Acrylic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scratch resistance | High | Moderate (gloss shows fine scratches) |
| Fingerprints | Low on matte | Shows readily on gloss |
| Heat tolerance | Good | Good |
| Repair if damaged | Easy to replace a shutter | Specialist work |
| Look over 10 years | Ages gracefully | Stunning, but needs more care |

Acrylic looks spectacular on day one and stays that way *if* you're happy to wipe it down regularly. In a high-traffic Chennai kitchen with kids, a quality matte laminate often ages more gracefully.

## Maintenance

Laminate — especially matte and textured — hides fingerprints and everyday smudges, and a normal wipe keeps it looking new. Acrylic's gloss shows every fingerprint and water spot, so it rewards a quick daily wipe. Neither is hard to maintain; they just ask for slightly different habits.

## Cost

Finish is one of the main levers on your total price. In our pricing model, finishes step up in tiers — quality **laminate** sits in the Essential tier, while **acrylic and membrane** finishes are part of the Premium tier, which carries a higher rate than Essential. The exact difference depends on how much cabinetry you're finishing.

## How to decide

Ask yourself three questions:

1. **How busy is the kitchen?** Heavy daily use and young kids tilt toward matte laminate.
2. **What look do you love?** If a deep gloss is the dream, acrylic on feature units is worth it.
3. **What's the budget priority?** Laminate frees up budget for better hardware and storage.
