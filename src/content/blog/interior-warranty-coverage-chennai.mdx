When investing in home interiors, most people focus on design, materials and budget. One equally important factor is routinely overlooked: **interior warranty coverage**.

A warranty gives you confidence that your investment is protected against manufacturing defects or workmanship issues for a defined period. Understanding what is covered — **and what isn't** — helps you choose an interior company with your eyes open.

## What is an interior warranty?

A written assurance from an interior company or product manufacturer that certain components will perform as expected for a specified period. Depending on the company and products, it may apply to modular kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, TV units, storage furniture, hardware, laminates, hinges and drawer channels — covering manufacturing defects and installation workmanship.

## Why it matters

A professionally executed interior is a long-term investment. A warranty protects it by providing support if covered issues arise after installation:

- Greater confidence in workmanship
- Protection against manufacturing defects
- Reduced repair costs for eligible issues
- Assurance of quality materials and installation

Companies that publish **clear** warranty terms are usually the ones confident in what they deliver.

## What a warranty typically covers

**Manufacturing defects.** If a cabinet panel, laminate or furniture component has a manufacturing defect, it may be repaired or replaced under warranty.

**Workmanship.** Most firms cover installation-related issues — misaligned shutters, improper fittings, hardware loose because of installation defects, drawer alignment and door adjustment problems.

**Modular furniture components.** Factory-manufactured units usually carry cover for structural defects arising from manufacturing or installation.

**Branded hardware.** Premium hardware makers frequently provide their *own* warranties on soft-close hinges, drawer channels, lift-up mechanisms and sliding systems. The duration depends on the manufacturer, not the interior firm.

## What is usually not covered

Most warranties exclude damage caused by external factors or improper use:

- Accidental damage, scratches and dents
- Water damage from plumbing leaks, flooding or fire
- Natural disasters
- Improper cleaning methods
- Unauthorised modifications
- Normal wear and tear
- Pest or termite damage (unless specifically covered)

Always read the warranty document carefully — the exclusions are where the substance is.

## Manufacturer warranty vs workmanship warranty

Homeowners often assume all warranties are the same. They fall into two distinct categories:

**Manufacturer warranty** — provided by the *product maker* for plywood, laminates, hardware and accessories. It typically covers manufacturing defects.

**Workmanship warranty** — provided by the *interior company* for the quality of installation and execution. It covers issues arising from improper installation rather than product defects.

<Callout>
Knowing which is which tells you **who to call**. A hinge that has lost its hydraulic tension is a manufacturer issue; a door that sags because the hinge plate was drilled a millimetre off is a workmanship issue.
</Callout>

## Questions to ask before you sign

- What warranty is provided on the modular furniture?
- Is workmanship covered, and for how long?
- Which hardware brands are being used?
- Do the branded accessories carry separate manufacturer warranties?
- What situations are excluded?
- How do I request warranty service?
- Is the warranty transferable if I sell my home?

Get the answers **in writing**. It prevents every misunderstanding that follows.

## Tips to maintain your warranty

- Clean surfaces with the recommended products.
- Wipe water spills promptly.
- Avoid overloading drawers and shelves.
- Repair plumbing leaks immediately.
- Keep interiors well ventilated.
- Avoid unauthorised repairs or modifications.

## Why quality materials matter more than the warranty

A warranty is valuable, but choosing premium materials from the start reduces the likelihood of ever needing it. For Chennai homes: **BWP or BWR plywood, moisture-resistant laminates, stainless steel hardware, premium edge banding and branded hinges.**

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is an interior warranty?
It varies by company, product and component manufacturer. Always request the written warranty details before signing. **Our own terms are published in full on our [warranty page](/warranty)** — coverage, exclusions and service response times.

### Are modular kitchens covered?
Many include cover for manufacturing defects and workmanship. Hardware components may also carry separate warranties from their makers.

### Does warranty cover water damage?
Generally, no. Damage from plumbing leaks, flooding or improper maintenance is usually excluded unless explicitly stated.

### Can I claim if I modify the furniture?
Unauthorised modifications or repairs may void the warranty. Consult your interior company before making structural changes.

## Final thoughts

A well-designed home is a significant investment, and understanding your warranty is part of protecting it. Review the terms, understand what's covered, and ask questions about workmanship, materials and after-sales support before you commit.
