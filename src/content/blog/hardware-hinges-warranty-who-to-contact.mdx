Most homeowners spend hours choosing plywood grades and laminates. But the true unsung heroes of an interior are the **moving parts** — the hinges, drawer channels, lift-up systems and sliding mechanisms you touch dozens of times a day.

Because they take the brunt of daily wear, one question inevitably arrives:

> *"If a hydraulic hinge loses its glide or a tandem drawer jams, who do I call — my interior firm, or the hardware manufacturer?"*

The answer depends entirely on **the root cause** and the terms of your coverage.

## Why hardware quality dictates lifespan

Cheap, unbranded hardware is the number-one point of failure in modular furniture. Premium brands (Blum, Hettich, Häfele) change the relationship you have with your home:

- **Cycle ratings** — premium hinges are rated for 40,000 to 200,000+ open/close cycles, which is 15–20 years of daily use.
- **Damping systems** — true soft-close protects heavy doors from cracking.
- **Load-bearing** — tandem boxes and pantry pull-outs carry 30–70 kg of heavy Indian cookware without sagging.

## Manufacturer vs installation warranty

| Warranty type | Who backs it | What it covers | Typical duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Manufacturing** | The hardware brand (Blum, Hettich, Häfele) | Internal mechanical failure, loss of hydraulic tension, material defects | 10 years to "lifetime functional" (often 15 years in India) |
| **Workmanship / installation** | Your interior firm | Door misalignment, screws loose from poor drilling, sagging hinges from bad balancing, levelling errors | Typically 1–2 years post-handover |

## What to do when hardware fails

1. **Check your invoice and BOQ.** Confirm the exact brand and model line installed (Hettich Sensys vs. Onsys, for example) to see whether an extended manufacturer warranty applies.
2. **Do a visual inspection.** Look at the hinge screws. Has the hinge physically slipped out of its alignment plate from vibration — or is the hydraulic cylinder leaking oil? **A physical shift is an adjustment issue; a fluid leak is a mechanical failure.**
3. **Contact your interior partner first.** A good firm sends a technician to determine whether it needs a screwdriver adjustment or a formal component replacement.
4. **Escalate to the brand if needed.** If your installation warranty has lapsed but the product warranty is live, get the technical inspection report from your designer and contact the brand's customer care.

<Callout>
Step 3 exists for a reason: **most "hardware failures" are alignment issues**, fixable in five minutes. Going straight to the brand often means waiting weeks for a call-out that concludes nothing was broken.
</Callout>

## What is excluded — always

Even lifetime-functional warranties carry real-world exclusions:

- **Corrosion and rusting** from aggressive cleaning sprays, spices or coastal humidity. Extended moisture exposure voids most hardware warranties.
- **Overloading stress** — packing a wire cutlery basket with heavy cast-iron pots.
- **Normal physical wear** — surface discolouration or scratches that don't alter operation.
- **Third-party modification** — repairs by uncertified carpenters rather than brand technicians.

## Care tips that avoid claims entirely

1. **Stop slamming shutters.** Let the soft-close mechanism do its job — forcing a hydraulic door shut ruins the fluid seal inside.
2. **Clear channel debris.** Vacuum sliding tracks and drawer runners every few months to remove crumbs, dust and lint.
3. **Wipe spills immediately.** If water drips from the countertop onto lower hinges, dry it with a microfibre cloth to avoid premature surface rust.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long is the standard Blum warranty in India?
For products purchased post-2022, Blum India offers a **15-year warranty** on standard mechanical hardware fittings (and 3 years on electrical components like SERVO-DRIVE), protecting against core manufacturing defects.

### Can I contact Hettich or Häfele directly?
Yes, you can reach their national customer-care lines. But it's usually faster to contact your original design firm first — we can verify whether it's a simple adjustment or a genuine brand claim, and coordinate on your behalf.

### Does an interior company's warranty cover rust on kitchen channels?
Generally, no. Rusting is almost always caused by environmental factors, cleaning chemicals or prolonged water exposure — home maintenance rather than installation defect.

## Final thoughts

The hardware behind your cabinets seems small, but it directly shapes the comfort of daily life. Knowing the difference between an alignment fix and a mechanical replacement means you can resolve issues quickly instead of chasing the wrong people.
