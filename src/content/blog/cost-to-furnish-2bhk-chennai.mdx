## What "furnishing a 2BHK" usually includes

When people picture a finished 2BHK, they're usually combining several separate pieces of work:

- **The fixed interiors** — modular kitchen, wardrobes, TV/entertainment unit, crockery and storage units. This is the core of the Essentials package.
- **The finishing layer** — false ceiling, cove lighting and painting. This transforms how a home feels and is the most common "add-on".
- **The loose layer** — sofa, dining set, beds, curtains and décor. Some clients bring these from their old home; others want them designed in.

Knowing which layer you're budgeting for is the single biggest source of "why are quotes so different?" confusion. A kitchen-and-wardrobes quote will always look cheaper than a turnkey one — because it's a different scope.

## Where the money goes

In a typical 2BHK, the rough split looks like this:

- **Kitchen** is usually the largest single line. See our [modular kitchen cost guide](/blog/modular-kitchen-cost-chennai) for what drives it.
- **Wardrobes** are the next big block — two bedrooms means two wardrobes, and finish choice matters here too.
- **TV unit and storage** are smaller but high-impact for the living room.
- **False ceiling, lighting and painting** are optional but change the whole feel of the home.

## How to phase it without compromising

You don't have to do everything at once. A sensible order:

1. **Kitchen and wardrobes first.** These are the hardest to add later and the ones you use every day.
2. **Living room next** — TV unit, storage and seating.
3. **Finishing layer last** — false ceiling, lighting and painting, once the core is in.

Because our units are factory-built, most 2BHK interiors install within the guaranteed 45-day timeline, and EMI options (up to 24 months, no-cost on select plans) let you spread the cost rather than phasing purely for budget reasons.
